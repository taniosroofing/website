Tile to Colorbond

Swap your tiles for steel

Concrete and terracotta tile roofs from the 1960s–90s are reaching the end of their life. Bedding cracks, tiles slip and leaks start. A Colorbond re-roof fixes the problem for good.

  • Old tiles, battens and bedding removed and taken away
  • Roof frame checked, with new metal battens where needed
  • New sarking and insulation blanket installed
  • New Colorbond sheeting, ridges and flashings
  • New gutters, fascia and downpipes available as part of the job
Metal to metal

Metal roof replacement

Old galvanised or rusting metal roofs replaced with new Colorbond sheeting, plus new fixings, flashings and ventilation.

  • Rusted sheets, screws and flashings replaced
  • Whirlybirds and roof ventilation fitted or replaced
  • Insulation upgraded while the roof is open
  • Stays weathertight at the end of every day
Why re-roof

Why go from tile to Colorbond?

No more cracked tiles

Steel sheets don't crack, slip or need re-bedding and re-pointing.

Lighter on your home

Far less weight on your roof frame than heavy concrete tiles.

Cooler and quieter

New sarking and insulation go in under every re-roof we do.

A modern look

A clean finish in the Colorbond colour you choose, with matching gutters.

Questions

Re-roofing FAQs

How much does a re-roof cost?

It depends on your roof's size, pitch, access and number of storeys. Most full re-roofs we do fall between $20,000 and $70,000+. We give you a fixed written price after we inspect your roof.

How long does a re-roof take?

It depends on the size of the roof and the weather. We give you a timeframe in your quote, and your home is made weathertight at the end of every day.

Do I need council approval?

Most re-roofs don't, but homes in heritage conservation areas may need approval to change from tile to metal. We'll check before we quote.

Can you keep my solar panels?

Yes. We can arrange for your panels to be removed and reinstalled by a licensed electrician as part of the job.
