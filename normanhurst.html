Re-roofing in Normanhurst

Tanios Roofing Group is a licensed NSW roofing contractor (Lic. 288958C) based in Sydney. We replace tired tile and metal roofs with new Colorbond roofs, including new sarking, insulation, flashings and gutters.

We do not publish generic star ratings or sample prices here. After a free inspection you get a fixed written quote for your roof, covering its size, pitch, access and the work it actually needs.

What we replace

Tile to metal, and metal to metal

Tile to Colorbond

Concrete and terracotta tile roofs that are cracking, slipping or leaking, replaced with new Colorbond sheeting.

  • Old tiles, battens and bedding removed and taken away
  • Roof frame checked, with new metal battens where needed
  • New sarking and insulation blanket installed
  • New Colorbond sheeting, ridges and flashings
  • New gutters, fascia and downpipes available as part of the job

Metal to metal

Old galvanised or rusting metal roofs replaced with new Colorbond sheeting, fixings, flashings and ventilation.

  • Rusted sheets, screws and flashings replaced
  • Whirlybirds and roof ventilation fitted or replaced
  • Insulation upgraded while the roof is open
  • Stays weathertight at the end of every day
How it works

From inspection to handover

  1. Inspect

    We inspect the roof at your Normanhurst property and take drone photos so you can see what we see.

  2. Quote

    You get a fixed-price written quote listing exactly what's included.

  3. Install

    Our crew installs your roof to manufacturer specs and Australian Standards, and keeps it weathertight at the end of every day.
