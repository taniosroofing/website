Re-roofing in Kellyville

Tanios Roofing Group is a fully insured ($20M public liability) roofing contractor based in Glenorie, in the Hills District. We replace tired tile and metal roofs with new Colorbond roofs, including new sarking, insulation, flashings and gutters.

Kellyville was semi-rural for most of the 20th century. Around 900 homes went up around Acres Road, known locally as The Village, between the 1960s and the 1980s, and the newer estates followed as the suburb grew. So a Kellyville roof could be an original tile roof now showing its age, or a much newer one on a smaller estate block. We’ll tell you honestly which one you have and what it needs.

Every re-roof we do comes with our 7-year workmanship warranty.

Kellyville roofs

Older streets and newer estates

Original tile roofs

On older homes around The Village, cracked tiles, failing ridge pointing and tired valleys are common reasons to re-roof. Swapping to Colorbond takes a lot of weight off the frame too.

Newer estate homes

If your home is newer, you probably don’t need a re-roof. If you’re adding a room or a second storey, we can build the new roof section to suit.

Tight side boundaries

Some newer estates have blocks of around 450–600 m², so access and edge protection sit close to the neighbours. We plan deliveries and set-up to keep driveways clear.

Creeks and trees

Homes near Kellyville’s creeks and bushland reserves collect leaves. New gutters, fascia and downpipes can be part of the job.

What we replace

Tile to metal, and metal to metal

Tile to Colorbond

Concrete and terracotta tile roofs that are cracking, slipping or leaking, replaced with new Colorbond sheeting.

  • Old tiles, battens and bedding removed and taken away
  • Roof frame checked, with new metal battens where needed
  • New sarking and insulation blanket installed
  • New Colorbond sheeting, ridges and flashings

Metal to metal

Old or damaged metal roofs replaced with new Colorbond sheeting, fixings, flashings and ventilation.

  • Rusted sheets, screws and flashings replaced
  • Whirlybirds and roof ventilation fitted or replaced
  • Insulation upgraded while the roof is open
  • Stays weathertight at the end of every day

Questions

Kellyville re-roofing FAQs

My house is fairly new. Do I need a re-roof?

Probably not. If we look at your roof and it doesn’t need replacing, we’ll tell you.

Do I need council approval?

Most like-for-like re-roofs don’t, but heritage listings and changes to the roof shape can. Kellyville is in The Hills Shire, and we check before we quote.

Can you match a roof for an extension?

Yes. We can roof a new extension or second storey in Colorbond, or re-roof the whole house at the same time so it all matches.

Can you keep my solar panels?

Yes. We can arrange for your panels to be removed and reinstalled by a licensed electrician as part of the job.
